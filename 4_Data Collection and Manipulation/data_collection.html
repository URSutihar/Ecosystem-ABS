NOT APPLICABLE (Hypothetical / Synthesised Data)

This simulation models a fictional ecosystem — its five biomes, twenty prey species, and six predator types do not exist in nature. There is therefore no real-world data source to collect from. All input parameters were derived from ecological reasoning and iterative calibration.
